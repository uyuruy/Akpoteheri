AKPOTEHERI - YOUR PORTRAIT GOES HERE

Upload your own photograph into THIS images folder.

Exactly name the file:
    profile-picture.jpg

So the final structure is:
    Akpoteheri/
      index.html
      images/
        profile-picture.jpg
        PHOTO_UPLOAD_INSTRUCTIONS.txt

The portrait on the homepage uses:
    <img src="images/profile-picture.jpg">

A JPG or JPEG image with that exact filename is recommended. If you have a PNG, convert it to JPG first, rather than just renaming the extension.

Photo suggestions:
- Clear professional headshot, facing front, neutral background
- Portrait/cropped image at least 500 x 600 pixels, preferably around 800 x 1000
- Avoid overly strong contrast and excessive retouching

No photograph is required to publish. Until this file exists, the card gracefully displays "ATU". Replacing the portrait later will not affect the other site.

IMPORTANT:
- DO NOT upload the photo to the Tony repository.
- Upload it to your NEW uyuruy/Akpoteheri repository, inside its /images/ folder.
- GitHub names and paths are case-sensitive on the website: match the filename exactly.
